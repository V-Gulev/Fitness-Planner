# Intelligent Fitness Planner

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

User-approved Java 21, Spring Boot 3.5.x with JUnit 5, Spring MVC, Thymeleaf, JPA, Maven Wrapper and local MySQL. No Docker or separate frontend.

## Users

People planning and recording resistance training with different experience, time and equipment constraints. The university student must also understand, demonstrate and defend the software and experimental findings.

## Product Purpose

Generate explainable weekly training plans and recommend progression from actual recorded performance. Compare the planning algorithm against a reproducible baseline for coursework.

## Capabilities and Constraints

The accepted scope is recorded in docs/PROJECT_BRIEF.md. Phase 4 supplies accounts, profiles, the 50-exercise library, explained plan generation, workout drafts/completed history and conservative progression with explicit target acceptance. Phase 5 adds a dashboard that uses the current plan and actual workout logs, plus per-exercise load and repetition charts with a table alternative. Phase 6 records a controlled offline comparison of the planning engines. Generated prescriptions and modeled volume must never be presented as completed activity. All shown controls must have a real destination or action.

## Brand Commitments

The supplied brief requests a dark or neutral athletic aesthetic, readable typography, consistent spacing, responsive layouts and restrained motion. Product name: Intelligent Fitness Planner. English UI and Bulgarian coursework were accepted as the proposed defaults.

## Evidence on Hand

The university brief, project master prompt and Phase 0 specifications exist. The planner comparison has measured software results from frozen fixtures. Workout walkthroughs use temporary synthetic records.

## Product Principles

- Explain decisions and unmet constraints.
- Keep prescribed training distinct from recorded performance.
- Preserve history when recommendations change.
- Prioritize readable, accessible task flows over decoration.
